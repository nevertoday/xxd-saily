A lot of people plan to sort it out when they get there. But picture the moment you land. You're standing in arrivals with no internet, the airport Wi-Fi won't connect, and the taxi queue is moving without you. Downloading an app, signing up, paying and installing an eSIM right then turns every step into a race.

There's a more concrete reason too. Saily warns that in Turkey, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and mainland China, the Saily app or website may be blocked, so if you're going to one of them, you need to install the eSIM before you travel ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164#travel)).

Installing early costs you nothing, because a <Term k="流量套餐" /> doesn't start the day you install it. It switches on by itself once you arrive in the country it covers and connect to a local network ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/21484628749084)). So this whole lesson comes down to one sentence:

<mark><strong>Install the eSIM at home on Wi-Fi. The plan only starts once you reach your destination.</strong></mark>

<Callout type="tip" title="The app follows your phone's language">
The Saily app follows your phone's system language ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/16929378623004)). The button names below are the English ones, as the Help Center writes them.
</Callout>

## Step 1: Install the Saily app

On an iPhone, open the App Store. On Android, open Google Play. Search for Saily and install it, or start from Saily's [download page](https://saily.com/download-esim-app/) ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/16927103076892)). The Saily app is only available for iPhone and Android.

## Step 2: Sign in

Open the app and sign in with your Google or Apple account. Saily doesn't have its own password, so you'll sign in with this same account every time ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/15698622789148)). The plans you buy are kept on that account, so reinstalling the app one day won't lose them.

## Step 3: Pick your destination and buy a plan

1. Tap **Explore plans**.
2. Choose the country or region you're traveling to.
3. Pick a plan, tap **Continue**, and pay.

When you pick, check first where it works. Saily sells plans by country or region, and a plan for one country won't connect in another ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164)). If your trip covers several countries, see whether there's a regional plan that bundles them together ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/13798863550492)).

Then look at the data. A standard plan gives you one total amount to use anytime during its validity period, not a daily amount. An Unlimited plan gives you a high-speed allowance each day, and once you've used it, your speed drops to about 1 Mbps for the rest of that day ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31092295169948)). Either way, whatever's left when the validity period ends is gone. If you're not sure which to pick, [step 1 of the full guide](/en/guide/1-choose-plan/) shows how to choose for your trip.

Prices change often, so this guide doesn't list them. Go by what the app shows. You can pay with a credit or debit card, PayPal, Apple Pay (iPhone only) or Google Pay, and checkout shows which ones you can use ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31030534656924)).

## Step 4: Install the eSIM on this phone

Before you start, connect to your home Wi-Fi and make sure you're not on a call, because an eSIM won't install during a phone call ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164#faq)).

1. In the app, tap **Set up eSIM**.
2. Choose **On this device**, tap **Continue**, then tap **Start eSIM install**.
3. Keep the app open. The install can take a few minutes, and your phone will show a few system prompts along the way. Answer them as below.

<Callout type="ok">
The Saily app tells you the eSIM is installed. In your phone's **Settings** > **Mobile Service** (the SIM screen on Android), there's a new line, which may be called Saily, Travel, or whatever you named it.
</Callout>

It won't connect yet, and that's normal. The plan only starts at your destination, so there's nothing to test before you go. Test it when you get there. If you find you've lost mobile data at home after installing, don't panic. See the first item under "If something's off" at the end of this page.

Once it's installed, leave it alone. <u>Above all, don't delete the eSIM in the Saily app.</u> Saily says that removes the eSIM and its plans from your account ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164#faq)).

That's it. The plan is bought and the eSIM is on your phone. Everything else happens after you land, and the next lesson has your phone use Saily for data and turns on data roaming for it.

## If something's off

<Q q="After installing, my phone has no mobile data at home.">

That's because installing switched your data line to Saily, and the Saily plan only works at your destination. If you still need mobile data before you leave, switch data back to your home SIM. On an iPhone, choose your home SIM under **Settings** > **Mobile Service** > **Mobile Data** (**Cellular Data** on some iPhones). On Android, choose it under **Mobile data** on the SIM screen. Lesson 3 switches it back to Saily after you land.

</Q>
<Q q="The app says the eSIM didn't install.">

Don't reinstall straight away. Check your phone's SIM settings first, because it may already be installed, and installing the same eSIM twice can cause errors. If it really isn't there, check your Wi-Fi and tap **Retry eSIM installation** in the app, up to 3 times. If it still fails, you can install it with a QR code or by typing the details in instead ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/15751399206172)). [Step 2](/en/guide/2-install/) of the full guide covers those.

</Q>
<Q q="It says the code is no longer valid, or already in use on another device.">

The eSIM may already be installed. Look in your phone's SIM settings for the Saily line first. If it's there, you don't need to install it again.

</Q>
<Q q="It says my phone is carrier locked.">

Your phone only accepts your carrier's SIMs. Ask your carrier to unlock it, then come back and install. [Lesson 1](/fr/start/1-check-phone/) shows how to check for a lock.

</Q>
<Q q="It says I've reached the eSIM slots limit.">

Your phone can only hold so many eSIMs. Remove one you no longer use in your phone's settings, then install from the app again.

</Q>
<Q q="My payment didn't go through.">

Don't keep retrying, or you may end up with duplicate charges or temporary holds. Check that you have enough funds and that the card details are right. If your bank declined it, contact them and ask them to allow payments to Saily ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/27480306670876)).

</Q>
<Q q="The Saily app or website won't open.">

Saily does warn that in Turkey, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and mainland China, the Saily app or website may be blocked ([Saily's note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164#travel)). This guide doesn't cover getting around network restrictions. If you're heading to one of those places, that's all the more reason to finish this lesson before you go.

</Q>
