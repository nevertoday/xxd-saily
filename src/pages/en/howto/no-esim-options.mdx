You read that Saily can give you a US number, so you bought it, opened the app, and found the next step is "Set up eSIM." But there's no Add eSIM anywhere in your phone's settings. Guides online say to "scan the code with another phone," and nobody you know has one to lend. Or you haven't bought your new phone yet and want to grab the number first. That's usually when the thought hits: did I buy the wrong thing?

Probably not. You just need to settle one question first. <mark>Saily's data and number both live on the same eSIM, and whichever device holds that eSIM gets the data and the texts. So first decide which device it's going on.</mark>

The number is an add-on that sits on the eSIM ([What is a Saily phone number](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/27948598334620)), and deleting that eSIM in the app cancels the number too ([official article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164)). The good news is that the Saily app itself isn't fussy. A phone without eSIM can still install the app, buy things and pass identity verification. Only the "install the eSIM" step gets stuck. Find your situation in the table below.

Not sure whether your phone supports eSIM? Look for **Add eSIM** in its settings. If it's there, the phone supports eSIM and isn't carrier-locked. You can also check Saily's [list of eSIM-compatible devices](https://saily.com/esim-supported-devices/). [Lesson 1](/en/start/1-check-phone/) goes into more detail.

## Situation 1: your phone supports eSIM, even if it's your only one

Lots of people think installing an eSIM always means scanning a code with a second phone. If your phone supports eSIM, you don't need a QR code at all. In the Saily app, tap **Set up eSIM**, choose **On this device**, tap **Continue**, then **Start eSIM install**. The app installs it and sets up things like data roaming along the way ([install guide](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/15751399206172)).

Stay on steady Wi-Fi while it installs, and turn off any VPN first. The full steps are in [Step 2: Install the eSIM](/en/guide/2-install/).

## Situation 2: the app can't install it, and there's no second device

When the automatic install fails, a **View instructions** button appears with the QR code, the SM-DP+ address and the <Term k="激活码" />. The code is on your own phone, and a phone can't scan its own screen. Saily gives two ways around that, and neither needs anyone's help ([official article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164#h_01M7MKZKE1X1VQ1NEG2A4V9VEY)):

- **iPhone: save it and pick it from Photos.** Screenshot the code, go to **Settings → Mobile Service → Add eSIM → Use QR Code**, tap **Open Photos**, and choose the screenshot.
- **Type it in.** On the scan screen, choose to enter the details yourself and type the SM-DP+ address and activation code exactly as shown. On Android, the activation code usually starts with `LPA:1$`.

There's one more option. The "second screen" doesn't have to be a friend's phone. Your own computer or tablet works. Send the screenshot to your own computer, open it, scan it with your phone, then delete the image. <u>Don't send the code to whoever's helping you or post it in a group chat. A QR code works for one install, and if someone else scans it first, you won't be able to install it.</u>

Where to scan on each kind of phone, and which switches to turn on afterwards, are in [Install the eSIM with a QR code, step by step](/en/howto/scan-qr/).

## Situation 3: your phone doesn't support eSIM

Plenty of phones don't support eSIM. Saily itself warns that many models sold in mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan and South Korea don't ([official article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164#travel)). If the phone has no eSIM chip, borrow one in card form. A <Term k="实体 eSIM 卡" /> looks like an ordinary SIM card and sits in the SIM tray, but it can hold an eSIM.

There are two ways to set one up:

- **Saily's way:** buy a card from eSIM.me, put it in place of your usual SIM, ask Saily support for a QR code, and load it following eSIM.me's instructions ([official article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12822397552028)).
- **What other users have done:** with cards like BeeSIM or 9eSIM, choose **On someone else's device** in the Saily app to show the QR code, screenshot it, then use the card's own mini-app or app to scan it and write it to the card over Bluetooth. <u>Saily's help center doesn't mention these cards, so using them is at your own risk. If something goes wrong, contact the card maker first.</u>

Neither way needs a second eSIM phone. Keep in mind that the card takes up a SIM slot, so on a single-slot phone you'll have to take your home SIM out. The details are in [Step 6: No eSIM on your phone](/en/guide/6-no-esim/).

## Situation 4: you don't have an eSIM device yet

Thinking of buying now and installing when the new phone arrives? It depends on what you're buying.

**If you only want the number, wait until the device arrives.** The number plan renews every period until you cancel it ([cancelling subscriptions](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31089593239452)). But the number sits on the eSIM, and until that eSIM is in a device, you can't receive texts, so you'd be paying for nothing. Once the phone or removable eSIM card is in your hands, buy the number and do the verification then.

**If you're buying a data plan, don't buy too early either.** Saily says you can install and activate a data plan within 180 days of purchase, and it activates automatically after that ([official article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/21484628749084)). Buying closer to the trip is the safest bet.

When you choose a device, check these three things:

1. **It's on Saily's list of compatible devices.** The list changes as new models come out, and support can depend on where the phone was sold. The same model can differ between a mainland China version and an overseas version ([device list](https://saily.com/esim-supported-devices/)).
2. **Its settings have Add eSIM.** Check in the shop, or as soon as you have it.
3. **It isn't locked to a carrier.** A locked phone can install the eSIM but can't connect with it. On iPhone, go to **Settings → General → About → Carrier Lock**. "No SIM restrictions" means it's unlocked ([official article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164)).

If you'd rather not change phones for this, take the Situation 3 route and add a removable eSIM card to the phone you have. An old spare phone that supports eSIM works too. You can keep it just for Saily and install it the same way as Situation 1.

<Callout type="tip">
When you get a new phone later, Saily can move over with you. See [Move the eSIM to a new phone](/en/howto/change-phone/). Before you move it, remember: delete it only in the old phone's settings, never with the delete option in the app, or your number gets cancelled too.
</Callout>

## How to tell it worked

Once it's installed, look in your phone's SIM settings for a new Saily line. With a removable eSIM card, check the same place after you put the card in. If you have a number, it shows at the top of the app's home screen once your identity verification is approved. Ask a friend to text it. If the text arrives, the number and your device are both working.

Keep the opening line in mind: decide which device it's going on, and the rest is following your situation. For the trickiest part, scanning the code, read [Install the eSIM with a QR code, step by step](/en/howto/scan-qr/) next.

## Stuck?

<Q q="I chose “On this device,” but my phone doesn't support eSIM, and now I can't find the QR code.">
Go back to this eSIM in the app, tap <strong>Set up eSIM</strong>, and this time choose <strong>On someone else's device</strong>. The QR code appears. If you still can't find it, contact Saily support and tell them you're putting the eSIM on a removable card (<a href="https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/17770364559516">how to contact support</a>).
</Q>
<Q q="I bought the number but don't have a device yet. Can I receive texts in the meantime?">
No. The number sits on the eSIM, and the eSIM has to be in a device for texts to arrive somewhere. If the device is a while away, think about cancelling the renewal for now. After you cancel, the number is held for 30 days. See <a href="/en/howto/keep-number/">Keep or change your number</a>.
</Q>
<Q q="Can I put the eSIM on a friend's phone and have them get my codes?">
A Saily eSIM works on one phone at a time (<a href="https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164">official article</a>), so on a friend's phone the codes only go to their phone, and the number can't be given to someone else either (<a href="https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31072966285724">official article</a>). Your own device or a removable card is the safer choice.
</Q>
