You've planned two weeks. Five days in Paris, the train to Rome, then a flight home from Barcelona. If you only buy a <Term k="流量套餐" /> for France, your phone goes offline the moment the train crosses the border, because that plan only works in France. When you finish this page, every stop on your trip will be covered by a Saily plan, and it'll all be installed before you leave.

With several countries, there's really only one thing to get right. <mark>A Saily plan only connects in the countries it covers, so first match every stop on your trip to a plan.</mark>

## Plans go by country, not by city

A Saily data plan covers either one country or one region. The official [troubleshooting guide](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164#h_01M7MKZKE117H2AA25C6YGR16B) puts it plainly. A plan for one country won't connect in another. Plans are also listed by country rather than by city, so to look up a city, search for its country ([coverage article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31091827466140)).

Tap **Explore Plans** in the Saily app, or open the [All Destinations](https://saily.com/all-destinations/) page on the Saily website, and pick a country to see its plans.

## One regional plan, or one per country

There are two ways to cover several countries.

The first is a **regional plan**. It bundles several countries into one plan, so one <Term k="eSIM" /> carries you through the whole trip and you don't buy again at each border. But not every country belongs to a region, and each regional plan only covers the countries on its own list, so look at the list before you buy ([regional plans article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/13798863550492#h_regional_plans_check_countries)). Here's how:

1. Open the [All Destinations](https://saily.com/all-destinations/) page on the Saily website.
2. Scroll down and select "Region."
3. Pick the region you want, then tap "View country list."

If every stop is on that list, this one plan is all you need.

The second is **one country plan for each country**. If a country you're visiting isn't in any region, Saily's advice is to try a different region or buy a plan just for that country. You can also mix the two, with a regional plan for most of the trip and a country plan for the one stop that isn't on its list. Which works out cheaper depends on the prices the app shows at the time.

If you fly all over the year round, Saily also has a Global plan, which is part of Saily Ultra. The official article says it covers 121 destinations ([coverage article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31091827466140#h_01M521KZDGPG1BS36Z4F260V0Y)).

## Look twice at islands and borders

Islands usually count as part of their country, but not always. Saily keeps a [table of islands and territories](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31091827466140#h_01M521SGWYJH5GSW2HK32RC5B2). For example, the Canary Islands use the Spain plan, Tahiti needs the French Polynesia plan, and Hawaii has its own "Hawaii (USA)" plan. The Galapagos Islands use the Ecuador plan, but coverage there is limited and service may come and go. If your trip includes islands, check them against that table before you leave.

Borders are a different story. Near one, your phone sometimes latches onto a neighboring country's network, and a weak one at that. Saily's fix is to turn off automatic network selection in your phone's settings and pick a local network by hand ([troubleshooting guide](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164#h_01M7MKZKE2SZ21YKQEVMD55EB2)).

## Install everything before you go

You can buy and install all your plans before the trip. Saily says that if your eSIM is installed before you travel, the plan waits and starts working automatically when you arrive at your destination ([data plan management](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/21484628749084#h_01K1JND27ZRVC02VZKP7JQVYCB)). Once it's installed, turn on <Term k="漫游" text="data roaming" /> for the Saily line too. It needs that switch to connect to local networks.

If your trip includes Turkey, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the UAE or mainland China, installing ahead matters even more. Saily warns that its app or website may not open in those places ([official note](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164#travel)). Turkey has one more catch. The Saily website and top-ups aren't available inside Turkey, so buy your plan before you enter the country ([activation article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/15751572038300#h_01KQYSVQNWB4DTM9CF03ZJ375C)).

## At each new stop, take a quick look

When you reach a new country, turn off Wi-Fi and open a website. If it loads, you're fine. If it doesn't, open the Saily app and check that the plan you're using covers the country you're in, which is also [a step](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164#h_01M7N1HJM2WDVNJA9V3DZW40W4) in Saily's troubleshooting guide. Your remaining data is shown in the middle of the app's home screen, under the eSIM.

Spend ten minutes before you leave matching each stop to a plan, and on the road all that's left is a quick look at each border.

## Stuck?

<Q q="The train crossed the border and my phone went offline.">
Open the Saily app and see where the plan you're using works. If it doesn't cover the country you're in now, buy a plan for this country in the app. Saily is clear that with the wrong plan, no setting will get you online. If the plan is right, your phone may still be on a neighboring country's network, so turn off automatic network selection and pick a local one. For anything else, go through [No signal, or signal but no internet](/en/howto/no-signal/).
</Q>
<Q q="The country I'm going to isn't in any region.">
Try a different region, or buy a country plan just for that country. That's Saily's own advice.
</Q>
<Q q="Can I call and text on a regional plan?">
No, regional plans are data only. To call and text across several countries, Saily says to add a Saily phone number to a Global plan. See the [regional plans article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/13798863550492#h_regional_plans_phone_number). For calling with the number, see [Make calls with the number](/en/howto/number-calls/).
</Q>
<Q q="I can't find my city in the list of plans.">
Plans are listed by country, not by city. Search for the country the city is in.
</Q>
