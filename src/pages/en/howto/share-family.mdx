A family of four is off to Japan. If everyone buys and installs their own plan, you end up keeping an eye on whose data is about to run out. Saily's shared plan, which it also calls the Family Plan, lets you buy one plan and bring your family in on it. When you finish this page, everyone in the family will have data on their own phone, all from the same plan.

<mark>If your family wants data on their own phones, buy a shared plan and invite them by email.</mark> If they only need a connection now and then, your hotspot is enough, and if it's just one person, you can buy a plan and give them its QR code. Here's each one in turn.

## One shared plan for up to five people

A shared plan lets you invite up to 4 more people, so 5 of you use the same plan's data. You're the plan owner. You invite people by email, and each of them claims the plan on their own phone ([shared plan article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/29753248333724)).

The data goes out to each person in small portions. Newly bought plans share it 256 MB at a time, which makes it feel closer to one pool everyone draws from. Plans that were already shared before this change still go out 1 GB at a time. If several people are sharing, Saily suggests choosing a bigger plan.

Not every plan can be shared, so check these before you buy:

- Ultra plans and Unlimited data plans can't be shared.
- You can only share a plan that hasn't been activated, which means nobody has installed it or started using it ([gifting article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31072966285724#h_01M500M4TEVA3P8YE686SZVP92)).
- 1 GB plans can be shared too, but you need Saily app version 3.63 or later.
- Sharing only works in the Saily app.

A shared plan keeps working until either all its data is used or it expires.

## Inviting your family

Your part is quick. Tap the plan on the Saily app's home screen, or go to **My plans** and select it, then add the other person's email address. They get an email with a link and claim the plan like this:

1. In the email, press **Claim plan**.
2. The Saily app opens on its own. Log in.
3. Confirm in the app that you want to use the plan.

They need the Saily app installed and a Saily account to log in with before they can claim it. Their phone also has to support <Term k="eSIM" /> and be unlocked, the same as when you installed your own.

## For one person, buy one and give it

Saily doesn't have a "gift" button, but you can buy a plan on your own account and send the eSIM's QR code to the other person ([gifting article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31072966285724#h_01M500M4TCRQ5VHQ7QT49VQ2DE)):

1. In the Saily app, choose the destination and <Term k="流量套餐" />, and pay as usual.
2. When the eSIM is ready, find its QR code in the app.
3. Send the QR code to them, for example as a screenshot in a private message.
4. They scan it on their own phone to install the eSIM.

<Callout type="warn">
Each eSIM installs on one device only, and a QR code can't be used again once it's been installed. So don't install it on your own phone first, and ask them to install it soon after they get it. Anyone with that QR code can install the eSIM, so send it only to the person it's for.
</Callout>

Before they install, their phone needs to support eSIM and be unlocked. If they use the Saily app, it has to be version 3.23 or newer, and they need a stable Wi-Fi connection for the install.

The plan stays linked to your account. A refund request goes through your account, and once the eSIM is installed or activated you may not get one. Gifting also covers data plans only, since Saily phone numbers and phone plans can't be given to someone else. And it's for personal use, because buying eSIMs to resell breaks Saily's Terms of Service.

One more question that comes up a lot. Your <Term k="抵用金" /> can't be moved to another account. If you want your credits to pay for a family member's trip, use them to buy a plan, then give the QR code as above.

## Just borrowing a connection? Use the hotspot

If a family member only needs to get online now and then, say to look up an address at a restaurant, your phone's <Term k="热点" /> is enough. A hotspot shares your phone's connection, the data comes out of your plan, and once you walk away they're offline. See [Share your data with a hotspot](/en/howto/hotspot/).

When you travel together and want everyone online on their own phone without keeping tabs on each person, a shared plan is the easy way. One plan, one person looking after it.

## Stuck?

<Q q="I can't find a way to share my plan.">
Check whether it's one of these. Ultra plans and Unlimited data plans can't be shared, a plan that's already been installed or used can't be shared, and 1 GB plans need Saily app version 3.63 or later. Sharing also only works in the app.
</Q>
<Q q="My family member pressed Claim plan and doesn't know what to do next.">
They need to install the Saily app and log in with a Saily account. Once they're logged in, they confirm in the app that they want to use the plan.
</Q>
<Q q="The person I sent the QR code to can't scan it.">
A QR code can't be used again once it's been installed, so think about whether someone already installed it, your own phone included. Saily says that if you installed it on your phone already, you should delete it there before passing it on. If it still doesn't work, contact Saily support. In the Saily app, open the **Help** tab and tap **Chat with us**.
</Q>
<Q q="Can I give my credits to a family member?">
No, credits can't be moved between accounts. You can use your credits to buy a plan and give them its QR code instead.
</Q>
