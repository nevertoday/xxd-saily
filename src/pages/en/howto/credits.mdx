A friend joins Saily with your referral code and makes a first purchase, and a few days later you notice some <Term k="抵用金" /> in your account. Your mom is going abroad next month, so you think you'll pass them on to her. You look everywhere, and there's no way to transfer them.

<mark>Credits only work in your own account, and they're valid for one year from the day they're issued.</mark> To use them for someone else, you spend them yourself and give that person the eSIM. Let's start with where they come from.

## Where credits come from

Credits are a balance in your Saily account that you can put toward a plan ([payment methods](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31030534656924)). Saily's help center mentions two sources.

One is **Refer a Friend**. Every Saily user has a referral code. When someone joins Saily with your code, they get a discount on their first purchase and you get Saily credit ([referral program](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/14262137198236)). The amounts are in the official article and the app, so this site leaves them out. The only referral code on this site is the author's own, on the [Quick number](/en/quick-number/) page. The discount is only for new users on their first purchase, and you can't use your own code ([gifting article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31072966285724)).

The other is Saily Ultra. With an Ultra subscription, you earn cashback in Saily credits on purchases, at the rate the official article gives ([What is Saily Ultra?](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/25455420492572)).

Coupon and voucher codes are a different thing, so don't mix them up with credits. You enter a code at checkout under **Got a coupon?**, and if it's still valid, it takes money off that order right there ([using a coupon code](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/15697852689052)).

## Where to find them and how to use them

Tap **Credits** at the bottom of the Saily app. Your referral code is on that screen. One of Saily's Chinese help articles calls this tab 积分 instead of 抵用金, but it's the same place.

On Android, you need to claim your referral reward by hand, using the **Claim Your Reward** option in the app ([gifting article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31072966285724)).

You can put credits toward a plan, but Saily's help center doesn't say at which step of checkout you apply them or how. This site won't guess. Go by what the app shows at checkout, and if you can't find where to use your credits, ask support. One more thing worth knowing: if you paid for part of a plan with credits or a discount code, Auto Top-Up later charges full price ([Auto Top-Up](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/20105542335132)).

## Using them for family

You can't move credits to another account. Saily's way around that is to buy an eSIM with your own credits, then send that eSIM's QR code to the other person, who scans it on their own phone to install it ([gifting article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31072966285724)). Two things to watch. Each eSIM installs on one device only, so don't install it on your own phone first. And gifting works for data plans only, since phone numbers and phone plans can't be given away. For how to gift one or share a plan, see [Share with your family](/en/howto/share-family/).

Think of credits as spare change in your account. You can only spend it in that account, it has to be spent within a year, and if you want someone else to have it, spend it on an eSIM first and give them that. For payment methods and invoices, see [Payment methods and invoices](/en/howto/payments/).

## Stuck?

<Q q="I want to transfer my credits to family.">
You can't. Buy an eSIM with your credits, send the QR code to them, and have them scan it on their own phone to install it.
</Q>
<Q q="My friend made a purchase, but my credit hasn't shown up.">
On Android, you claim referral rewards yourself. Look for **Claim Your Reward** in the app. If it's still missing, ask support.
</Q>
<Q q="Why did Auto Top-Up charge full price?">
If you paid for part of that plan with credits or a discount code, Saily says Auto Top-Up charges full price.
</Q>
