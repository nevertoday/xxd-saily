You bought a plan for Seoul next month, and then the trip fell through. Or your thumb slipped at checkout and you picked the wrong country. Whether you get your money back comes down to one thing for Saily. When you finish this page, you'll know whether this purchase can be refunded and where to ask, and you'll have stopped any subscription that's still charging you.

<mark>A plan that isn't installed or activated can get a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Once it's installed and activated, Saily counts it as used.</mark>

## Not installed or activated? Full refund within 30 days

Saily's refund policy says that if the <Term k="eSIM" /> you bought hasn't been installed, or the <Term k="流量套餐" /> hasn't been activated, you can ask for a full refund within 30 days of purchase ([refund policy](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/16420576170652)). A plan normally starts once you're in the country it covers, so if you haven't left yet, it probably hasn't activated.

Saily's article on gifting puts it more cautiously, though. It says that once the eSIM is installed or activated, you may not get a refund ([gifting article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31072966285724#h_01M500QP7B2KA9QJSR7QCGDXBC)). So if your plans aren't settled yet, hold off on installing. Once the trip is certain, install, then go.

## Already activated? Saily considers two cases

Once you've installed the eSIM and activated the plan, Saily treats it as used and can't promise a full refund. Still, Saily says it may consider a partial or full refund in two cases:

- You couldn't use much of the data because of a quality problem on Saily's side.
- The plan is activated, but you've barely touched it and more than 99% of the data is left.

The exact terms are in the refund section of Saily's [Terms of Service](https://saily.com/legal/terms-of-service/).

If the eSIM is installed but you've never been able to get online, don't rush to a refund. Saily says signal without internet usually comes down to settings, so go through [No signal, or signal but no internet](/en/howto/no-signal/) first. If it really is Saily's network, that's the first case above, and your request should say which steps you tried.

## Where to ask

Saily says the fastest, most direct way is to [submit a refund request on its website](https://saily.com/auto-refund-center/?flow=mobile). You can also contact support. Open the **Help** tab in the Saily app and tap **Chat with us** ([contacting support](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/17770364559516)). A plan you gave someone stays linked to your account, so the refund request goes through your account too.

## Stop subscriptions yourself

A refund covers money you've already paid. Whether you keep getting charged is a separate question. Saily Ultra, phone number plans and auto top-up all charge on a cycle, and you have to turn them off in the app yourself. After you cancel, you keep the benefits until the current billing period ends ([cancelling subscriptions](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31089593239452)).

**Cancel Saily Ultra.** Open the Saily app, go to **Profile** > **Your plans**, select your Ultra plan, cancel it and confirm. The app may first offer you a lower-tier plan. If you take it, it starts at your next billing cycle, and Saily says you won't be charged again. If you still want to cancel, the plan pauses for one billing cycle and then ends for good. If you can't cancel in the app, contact support and they'll cancel it for you.

**Cancel a phone number plan.** In the same place, **Profile** > **Your plans**, select the <Term k="电话号码套餐" /> and cancel it. After the plan ends, your number is held for 30 days. To keep it, tap **Get number** in the app and buy the phone number plan again on the same eSIM. After 30 days the number is released and can't be recovered. For more on the number, see [Keep or change your number](/en/howto/keep-number/).

**Turn off auto top-up.** Go to the **eSIMs** tab, select the eSIM, tap **Top-up**, then **Cancel auto top-up** and confirm. When the green dot on the **Top-up** button disappears, it's off.

Whether you get a refund depends on whether the plan was installed and activated. Whether you keep getting charged depends on whether your subscriptions are off. Check the two separately and nothing slips through.

## Stuck?

<Q q="My trip was cancelled and I haven't installed the plan.">
That's exactly the case for a full refund, as long as it isn't installed or activated and it's within 30 days of purchase. [Submit a refund request](https://saily.com/auto-refund-center/?flow=mobile) on Saily's website.
</Q>
<Q q="The eSIM is installed, but it's never worked.">
Go through [No signal, or signal but no internet](/en/howto/no-signal/) first. If it still doesn't work, contact support and tell them where you are, which phone you have and what you've tried. Not being able to use your data because of a quality problem on Saily's side is one of the cases Saily says it considers for a refund.
</Q>
<Q q="I can't find where to cancel a subscription in the app.">
Saily says that if you can't cancel an Ultra plan in the app, support will cancel it for you. For the other subscriptions, ask support if you can't find the option.
</Q>
<Q q="I want to pay for my phone number with a different card.">
Saily's way is to cancel the phone number plan, let it expire, and buy it again with the new card. Your number is held for 30 days in between, so don't leave it longer than that.
</Q>
