You've probably signed up for a few apps with this US number by now. The day you get a new phone and have to log in again, the code goes to this number, and if it isn't yours anymore, you may not get back into those accounts. So rather than finding out the hard way, it's worth knowing what keeps it yours.

<mark>The number follows your plan and your account, so while the plan keeps renewing and the eSIM and your account stay in the app, it's yours.</mark> On the other side, cancelling, deleting and changing numbers can each take it away from you. Some of those you can undo, and some you can't.

## What keeps it yours

Saily says that for as long as your service stays active, the number is assigned only to you ([official article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/27948689277596)). The <Term k="电话号码套餐" /> is a subscription that renews every billing period, so it keeps going until you cancel it. It's also yours alone. Saily says you can't share or give Saily phone numbers or phone plans to someone else ([gifting article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31072966285724)).

The other thing to get done first is <Term k="KYC 实名认证" />. Telecom and anti-money-laundering rules require Saily to confirm you're the legitimate holder of your ID before it switches on the number. Until that's done, the number isn't shown to you or activated, and if Saily later asks you for KYC information and you don't provide it, it may stop providing the number ([KYC requirements](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/29288111980060)). When you get ready to verify, these are the points from that article that trip people up most:

- In countries without a one-tap provider, you can use a national ID card (both sides), a passport (photo page) or a driver's license (both sides). It has to be a valid, government-issued, physical document. Photocopies, screenshots and photos of a document on another screen aren't accepted.
- Some countries have stricter rules. <u>If your document was issued in China, you need to submit both a passport and a national ID card</u>, and one alone won't complete verification.
- Residence permits, visas and a few other document types aren't accepted from any country.
- Photograph your document on a flat, dark, solid-colored surface, with all four corners in the frame, no glare and no fingers over it. Besides the document, you may be asked for a selfie or a short video.

## What loses it

Four things can take the number away from you, and Saily only describes a way back for the first:

- **Cancelling the phone number plan.** You keep the number until the current billing period ends. After the plan ends, the number stays reserved for 30 days. Within those 30 days, tap **Get number** in the app and buy the phone number plan again on the same eSIM to keep it. After 30 days the number is released and can't be recovered ([cancelling](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31089593239452)).
- **Deleting the eSIM inside the Saily app.** That's different from deleting it in your phone's settings. Deleting it in your phone's settings only takes it off that phone, and your plans stay in your Saily account. Using *Delete eSIM* in the Saily app removes the eSIM and its plans from your account and cancels its phone number too ([official article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12838788763164)). See also [Read this before you delete an eSIM](/en/howto/delete-esim/).
- **Deleting your Saily account.** That's permanent, and you lose access to everything you've bought ([deleting your account](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/27480241534620)).
- **Changing your number.** Once the new number arrives, the old one can't be recovered. More on that below.

## Stopping it, or paying with a different card

To stop the phone number plan, open the Saily app, go to **Profile**, tap **Your plans**, select the phone number plan and cancel it.

To pay with a different card, Saily's advice is to cancel the plan, wait for it to expire, then buy it again with the new card ([cancelling](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31089593239452)). Put that together with the 30 days above. After the old plan expires the number is still reserved for 30 days, so buy it again on the same eSIM with the new card soon after, and you keep the same number.

## Changing to a new number

Saily lets you change your number once for free in the app if you're not receiving verification codes, you're getting unwanted calls, or the number is already registered with another service. After that free change, you can contact support to ask for one more. These steps follow the [official article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/27948598334620#h_saily_phone_number_change):

1. On the **Home** screen, tap the eSIM selector at the top, where your eSIM name and current number appear.
2. In the **eSIMs** list, tap the three-dot button next to the eSIM whose number you want to change.
3. Under **Phone number**, tap **Change number**.
4. Pick your reason, read the warning, and tap **Change number**.
5. Tap **Done**.

The new number can take up to 24 hours to be assigned, and you can see its status at the top of the Home screen. Your current number keeps working until the new one is ready, but once it's been replaced you can't get the old one back, and the free in-app option won't appear again.

<Callout type="warn" title="Before you change">
Once the old number is gone, it doesn't come back. First move the important accounts tied to it over to a way of verifying that doesn't rely on texts, like an authenticator app or email, then make the change.
</Callout>

## One user's habit: keep the account and the card alive

Saily's help center doesn't say what happens to the number when a renewal payment fails. One user on X shares how he holds on to his ([original post](https://x.com/AI_Jasonyu/status/2080877134319624492)). He says not to uninstall the Saily app or lose your Saily account, because the number is managed in the app. He also says to keep the card it bills from valid, funded and not blocked by your bank, since in his view a failed charge could get the number paused or even taken back. So every few months he opens the app and checks the subscription status. That's his experience, and the official docs come first.

In short, while the plan renews, the eSIM stays in the app and your account exists, the number stays yours. Before you stop or change it, think about which accounts still depend on the old one. For how call minutes work, see [Make calls with the number](/en/howto/number-calls/).

## Stuck?

<Q q="I cancelled and now I want it back.">
Within 30 days of the plan ending, tap **Get number** in the app and buy the phone number plan again on the same eSIM. That keeps the number. After 30 days it can't be recovered.
</Q>
<Q q="I can't find the option to change my number.">
The free change only happens once, and after you've used it the option no longer appears. To change again, ask support. Saily says you can request one more change.
</Q>
<Q q="My KYC keeps failing.">
Check the document rules. A Chinese-issued document needs both a passport and a national ID card. Photocopies, screenshots and photos of a screen aren't accepted. All four corners need to be in the frame, without glare. The full requirements are in the [official article](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/29288111980060).
</Q>
