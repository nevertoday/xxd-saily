Plenty of people come to Saily without any trip planned. They just want a +1 US number for verification codes. Then they open Saily, see data plans for every country, and assume they have to buy data first and get a number on the side. You don't. <mark>Saily's website sells the number on its own: pick yearly, quarterly or monthly, click Get Number Only, check out and pay, and the number subscription is yours.</mark>

The red arrows on each screenshot show where to click, numbered in order. It all happens in a browser, on a computer or a phone, and the screenshots follow your screen: on a computer you see the desktop site, on a phone the mobile one.

## Step 1: Open the phone number page and click Get a Phone Number

Go to Saily's [phone number page](https://saily.com/esim-phone-number/) and click the black **Get a Phone Number** button. Every page on saily.com also has the same button at the top right, and it does the same thing.

Under the price there's a line of small print: after you buy, you verify your identity in the Saily app. Keep that in mind; we'll come back to it at the end.

## Step 2: Pick how often it renews, then click Get Number Only

A window pops up asking how often the number subscription renews: **Yearly**, **Quarterly** or **Monthly**. The longer the period, the less it costs per month; the window shows the current prices. Whichever you pick renews automatically until you cancel, and your number stays the same for as long as you keep renewing.

This next click is the one that matters. There are two buttons at the bottom. The yellow **Continue** takes you on to pick a calls-and-texts plan as well, and the white **Get Number Only** buys just the number. If all you want right now is the number, click the white one. You can buy minutes and texts later, when you need them; [step 5 of the full guide](/en/guide/5-phone-number/) explains how they're charged.

Can a number on its own still receive texts and verification codes? The Help Center doesn't say. One user on X reports that receiving texts costs nothing ([the post](https://x.com/AI_Jasonyu/status/2080877134319624492)), but that's their experience, not Saily's word.

## Step 3: Add a referral code at checkout

Next comes the checkout page. **Your account** comes first (the left column on a computer); if you're not signed in, sign in or create a Saily account there. Below it is the **Order summary** (the right column on a computer): check that **Type** says **Phone number** and **Renews** shows the period you just picked.

Then click **Got coupon code?** under the order summary, paste in this referral code, and click **Apply**:

It opens a box where you type the code, with an **Apply** button next to it.

The referral code is Saily's own Refer a Friend program: the Help Center says a new user who joins with someone's referral code gets $5 off their first purchase ([referral program](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/14262137198236)). It only works on a new user's first purchase, and you can't use your own code ([gifting an eSIM](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31072966285724)). So after you click **Apply**, look at **Total today**. If it went down, the code worked. If it didn't, you've most likely bought from Saily before; just pay the full price and you'll still get the number.

To be upfront: this is the referral code of the person who runs this site. If you use it, they get some Saily credit, which is how Saily's referral program works. You're under no obligation; leave the box empty and the purchase works the same.

## Step 4: Choose a payment method and pay

Pick a way to pay under **Select a payment method** (the left column on a computer). Saily takes cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay, though some methods aren't available everywhere, so go by what checkout lists ([payment methods](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/31030534656924)). If a payment is declined, see [Payment methods and invoices](/en/howto/payments/).

<Callout type="warn" title="After you pay, there's still the ID check">
You get the number only after you verify your identity. The Help Center says Saily won't show you which number you've been given, or activate it, until you pass (<a href="https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/29288111980060">official article</a>). You do this in the Saily app, and if your ID was issued in China, you need both your passport and your national ID card. For which documents count and how to photograph them so they pass, see <a href="/en/guide/5-phone-number/">step 5 of the full guide</a>.
</Callout>

Once the number is yours, the thing that matters most is not letting it lapse: if renewal stops, the number is held for only 30 more days. For keeping it and changing it, see [Keep or change your number](/en/howto/keep-number/); for using it with verification codes, see [Texts and verification codes on the US number](/en/howto/us-number-sms/).
